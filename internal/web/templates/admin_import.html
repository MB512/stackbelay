Move a stack here from another host (or rebuild a host): choose or enter the repository that holds its snapshots. StackBelay lists the stacks in it; the one you choose is added with that repository, without a directory, without schedules and visible to admins only. Its Backups tab then lists the snapshots of the chosen host, and its first restore creates the stack directory under the same name. The name cannot be changed on the way.
