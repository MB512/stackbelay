Five fields: minute, hour, day of month, month, day of week (e.g. 0 3 * * * = daily at 03:00, 30 1 * * 1-5 = weekdays at 01:30), or @daily, @weekly, @every 6h. Times are in the manager's time zone, {{.TimeZone}} (set with TZ). Runs missed while the manager is down are not repeated.

Job

A scheduled update pulls the images of the running services and only continues if one of them is new (or runs a version pinned by a restore): then it backs up, recreates and verifies the services and rolls back if they do not come up. It needs a running stack; without repository it runs without backup and without rollback, which you have to confirm below.

{{- if not .Repo.Exists}}

This stack has no repository. A scheduled update then runs without backup and without rollback: a failed update leaves the stack as it is, not even the old images are started. Snapshot and rollback options are ignored. Once the stack has a repository, its updates are backed up again.

{{- end}}
