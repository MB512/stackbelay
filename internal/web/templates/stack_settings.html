Source host

The Backups tab lists and restores the snapshots of this host. Choose another host to move this stack here from there: restore its snapshots (again and again, in place, while it still runs there), then the last one after its final backup, and switch back to this host. Only admins may restore another host's snapshots. Backups are always made under this host's name. Other hosts are offered once the snapshot list contains their snapshots.

Job

A scheduled update pulls the images of the running services and only continues if one of them is new (or runs a version pinned by a restore): then it backs up, recreates and verifies the services and rolls back if they do not come up. It needs a running stack; without repository it runs without backup and without rollback, which you have to confirm below.
